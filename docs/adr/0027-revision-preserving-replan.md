# Re-planning appends a schedule revision to the same Daily plan

Once a Daily plan has any work outcome, regeneration and deletion are refused. Re-planning the current day appends a schedule revision instead of deleting the plan: earlier planned intervals and outcomes stay available, and only the latest revision is actionable. The revision reuses the plan's captured scheduling snapshot, reloads that date's commitments, and starts a new focus stretch without recording a restorative break. The client supplies the current date and cutoff; persistence rechecks the expected revision and effort versions after the provider call, so a stale re-plan cannot overwrite progress saved while ranking was in flight.

Replacing the whole plan would erase the actuals needed for planned-versus-actual comparison. Keeping the old row only long enough to preserve outcomes, while discarding its future schedule without a revision, would remove the context those outcomes were recorded against. A legacy plan without a complete scheduling snapshot cannot take this path, and current preferences must not be invented as its historical settings. Predictive warnings about a future due date are also outside this decision; 1.3.0 only warns about due or overdue work that does not fit the day being planned.
